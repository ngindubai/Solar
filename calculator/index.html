Sixty seconds, your numbers

What would solar actually save you?

Answer a few questions about your home or business. We estimate your DEWA band, then show both routes side by side: free solar under a PPA, and buying your system outright.

How it works: we walk your consumption through DEWA's slab tariffs plus the fuel surcharge, then remove the most expensive units first, because that is what solar does. Assumptions are optimistic but stated: Dubai yield of about 1900 kWh per kWp per year, PPA rate 25% below your blended displaced rate, indicative turnkey pricing of AED 3,000/kWp for homes and AED 2,400/kWp at commercial scale. Verify tariffs at dewa.gov.ae. Every figure is an estimate, not a quote. Final numbers depend on a site survey and DEWA approval.
