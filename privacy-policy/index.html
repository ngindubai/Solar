Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. Last updated 23 July 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by {{BRAND_NAME}}, a solar PV and battery storage installer based at Unit 20, Prime Tower, Business Bay, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We work by web form and email only; you can reach us at garethsomers@outlook.com.

What the forms collect

The forms on this site collect your name and email address, together with any optional information you choose to provide. Depending on the form, the optional fields may include:

  • whether your enquiry is for a home or a business (segment);
  • your emirate or area;
  • your approximate monthly bill range;
  • a free-text message; and
  • a summary of your results from the savings calculator, if you include one.

We collect nothing else through the forms, and we never ask for a contact number.

Why we collect it

We use the information you send for one purpose: responding to your enquiry and preparing your solar and battery storage estimate. We do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Legal basis

We process personal data in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL). Our basis for processing is your consent, given when you submit a form, together with the steps needed to respond to the enquiry you have made.

How your submission reaches us

Forms on this site are processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-handling service that relays each submission to our email address. FormSubmit receives the contents of the form in order to deliver it and operates under its own terms and privacy practices. Please avoid putting sensitive personal information in the message field.

No sale of data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Retention

We keep your details only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any work that follows from it. Correspondence that leads nowhere is deleted.

Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted. To exercise any of these rights, email garethsomers@outlook.com and we will respond by email.

Cookies and analytics

We set no cookies, and no analytics tools are currently active on this site. If that changes, this policy will be updated before anything goes live.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. This version was last updated on 23 July 2026.
