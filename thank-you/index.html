What happens next

Three steps, all by email

1

We review your details

An engineer reads your enquiry and works through what you have told us about your property or premises, your bill and your interest in free solar (PPA) or buying.

2

Your tailored estimate

You receive an email comparing both routes — free solar under a PPA and buying outright. Estimates are indicative, not a quote; final figures depend on a site survey and DEWA approval.

3

Optional free site survey

If the numbers look right to you, we arrange a free survey of your roof to firm the figures up before anything is signed. No obligation at any point.
